### GPT-Pro

Technologies: ReactJS, NodeJS, OpenAIGPT-Pro is a chrome extension to write upwork proposals and answer questions on Upwork proposals based on your profile. You can also get the upwork job alerts based on your pre-defined job criteria with real-time chrome notifications. Moreover, you can get the job statistics (by demographics, categories, time) in the form of charts using this extension.
